See It In Action

From first smoke
to fire out.

Scroll to watch a clean agent system protect an electrical room — the kind of system I specify, propose and see through to commissioning.

  1. 01 Detect

    Smoke from an overheating switchgear panel reaches the ceiling detectors. Typically two detectors must confirm the fire before the system acts, to avoid false discharges.

  2. 02 Alarm

    Strobes and sounders warn people to leave. The release panel starts a pre-discharge countdown and shuts down ventilation so the agent stays in the room.

  3. 03 Release

    FM-200 flows from the cylinders through the pipe network and out of the nozzles — within 10 seconds, as NFPA 2001 requires.

  4. 04 Extinguish

    The agent reaches design concentration and breaks the combustion chain. The fire is out — no water, no residue, and the equipment is back in service fast.

How I Work

Consultative
by design.

01

Discover the risk

Site visits and stakeholder interviews with end users, consultants and contractors to understand the hazard, the asset at risk, operational constraints and the specification before any product is proposed.

02

Engineer the fit

Translate the requirement into a compliant design basis — suppression agent and method, pump duty point and driver, detection technology and coverage — referenced to NFPA and Aramco / Saudi Energy / Civil Defense requirements.

03

Advise on options

Compare technical options, OEM alternatives and lifecycle cost so the client and consultant can decide; value-engineer and substitute equivalent approved products when budget or delivery demands it.

04

Stay through delivery

Remain the client’s technical point of contact through submittals, consultant approvals, delivery, testing and commissioning — building the trust that drives repeat business.
